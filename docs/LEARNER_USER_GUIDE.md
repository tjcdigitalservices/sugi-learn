# SugiLearn — Learner User Guide

**Audience:** Learners using SugiLearn  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-15

A simple guide to using SugiLearn. Ask your instructor if you need login details.

---

## 1. Login

1. Open the SugiLearn website address provided by your instructor.
2. Go to **Login**.
3. Enter your email and password.
4. You will arrive at your **Home** page.

To sign out, use **Sign out** in the top navigation.

---

## 2. Pre-Assessment

Before you begin the chapters, you may be asked to complete a **Pre-Assessment**.

1. From the menu, choose **Pre-Assessment**.
2. Read each question carefully.
3. Select one answer per question.
4. Use **Previous** and **Next** to review your answers.
5. Click **Submit** when finished.

If Pre-Assessment is “not available yet,” your instructor may still be preparing questions. Check back later.

---

## 3. Learning Journey

Your **Home** page shows your overall progress and what to do next.

---

## 4. Chapters

When you open a chapter:

- Read each section in order (story, illustrations, characters, learning points, etc.)
- Use **Previous** and **Next** at the bottom to move between chapters
- At the end, click **I've finished this chapter** to save your progress

Your place is saved automatically as you progress.

---

## 5. Progress

Go to **Progress** to see:

- How many chapters you have completed
- Which chapters are in progress or not yet started

You can return to any available chapter at any time.

---

## 6. Post-Assessment

After completing the chapters (or when your instructor allows), take the **Post-Assessment**.

1. Choose **Post-Assessment** from the menu.
2. Answer all questions the same way as the Pre-Assessment.
3. Submit when finished.

If it is not available yet, you may need to finish more chapters or complete the Pre-Assessment first.

---

## 7. Results

Go to **Results** after you finish the Post-Test to see:

- **Learning Results** — Pre-Test, Post-Test, and Learning Gain cards
- A question-by-question grid of correct and incorrect answers
- **Review Your Answers** — for each incorrect question, your answer, the correct answer, and an explanation
- **Print PDF** / **Share PDF** — a learner assessment report with your name and scores

Scores are shown as a percentage. Your instructor can explain what the results mean for your program.

---

## Navigation Tips

- On a phone or tablet, tap the **menu** icon (☰) to open navigation.
- If a page does not load, try again or contact your instructor.
- Do not share your password with others.

---

## Need Help?

Contact your instructor or program administrator. They can reset passwords, check your progress, and tell you when new chapters become available.
